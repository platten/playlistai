# Composer-credit requests

“Composed by” names a recording's composer, not its displayed performer. The intent parser retains the source wording as a required `composer` criterion. Candidate discovery searches the indexed track-level `composer` tag, and every selected recording must have an affirmative, non-conflicting composer credit. Artist names, titles, album-level composer tags, CLAP similarity, and uncredited recordings cannot satisfy the criterion. If there are too few verified recordings, generation returns fewer tracks or a clarification/partial outcome rather than padding with music by unrelated composers.

This is a metadata contract, not an inference from audio. Deej-AI-only reports composer requests as unsupported because it does not inspect recording credits. Spelling variants that do not match a catalog composer credit may need correction or an alias-supported source; the app must not silently substitute a performer for an unknown composer. Saved intent keeps the criterion and source evidence for replay.

An offline local check on the installed shared discovery pack used `Relaxing Classical music composed only by Fryderyk Chopin` with the rules parser, Enhanced Hybrid, isolated temporary stores, and no model or preview analysis. It selected 20 recordings in 16.3 seconds; all 20 selected IDs had the exact indexed `composer: Fryderyk Chopin` posting. This verifies that run's field-level contract, not held-out musical quality or default-model parsing. The normal fast regressions use synthetic composer tags and do not require the installed pack.
